================================================================================
WHAT DATA WE STILL NEED, AND WHY
Written 2026-09-14, after Experiment 1
================================================================================

SHORT VERSION

  One new render corpus is worth commissioning, and it is a variation on
  selfws_v2 rather than a new idea: the no_kitchen PROTOCOL run inside FURNISHED
  kitchens, at 4x the scene count, with more arms, several cameras per scene, and
  the two label types the simulator can emit for free and currently does not
  (segmentation masks and object poses).

  But do not commission it yet. The scene-count scaling curve is running on
  existing data right now and it decides the question. Section 0.

  A second, cheaper thing is needed and is not a render at all: an EVALUATION
  harness for held-out embodiment x held-out task rollouts. Without it the policy
  result stays a training-loss statement.


================================================================================
0. THE SCALING CURVES -- MEASURED, NOT GUESSED
================================================================================
  The only structural difference between eef_pairs (failed) and selfws_v2
  (worked: 0.838 retrieval on real frames) is a SCENE AXIS -- 32 distinct
  RoboCasa kitchens, verified distinct (max pairwise scene similarity 0.608).
  How many scenes that takes, and how many arms, had never been measured.
  Both curves were run before writing the rest of this document.

  NOISE FLOOR FIRST. Five runs of the IDENTICAL configuration (24 scenes,
  4 arms, method D) across seeds and budgets:
      EEF rotation   mean 20.9 deg   sd 1.1    range 19.6 - 22.7
      EEF translation mean 15.2 cm   sd 0.9    range 13.9 - 16.3
      pose discrimination mean +0.31 sd 0.04   range +0.26 - +0.36
  Every claim below is read against those.

0.1 SCENE COUNT -- THE DOMINANT AXIS, STILL FALLING AT OUR MAXIMUM
  Method D, pooled over two budgets (1,200 states and 350 states; the 350 budget
  binds at every point, so that arm of the curve varies DIVERSITY ONLY, with
  volume held at ~340 states).

      scenes    EEF rotation        factor vs previous
      ------    ------------        ------------------
         1        112.9 deg
         2        112.6 deg              1.00x
         4         97.0 deg              1.16x
         8         53.1 deg              1.83x
        16         32.2 deg              1.65x
        24         20.9 deg  (n=5)       1.54x
      frozen      100.4 deg

  Monotonic, and the 16 -> 24 step alone is 11 deg = ten times the replicate sd.
  Nothing has flattened. Extrapolating the ~1.5x per doubling: 48 scenes ~14 deg,
  96 scenes ~9 deg. Diminishing, but real and large.

  TWO THINGS THIS SETTLES.
  * At FOUR OR FEWER KITCHENS the rotation error is 97-113 deg, WORSE than the
    untrained backbone's 100.4 -- and that is method D, which has the explicit
    EEF term. Scene diversity, not the loss, is what stops alignment destroying
    state information. eef_pairs had ZERO scenes; this is why it failed.
  * Pose discrimination saturates by ~8 scenes (+0.31 at 8, +0.31 at 16, +0.36 at
    24, all within 1-1.5 sd). Scenes beyond 8 buy METRIC EEF ACCURACY, not
    embodiment invariance. The two properties come from different axes.

0.2 ARM COUNT -- REAL, MONOTONIC, AND MUCH WEAKER
  All 24 scenes, pooled over two seeds.

      arms    pose discrimination     EEF translation
      ----    -------------------     ---------------
        1        +0.029 (n=2)            21.5 cm
        2        +0.180 (n=2)            17.2 cm
        3        +0.218 (n=1)            16.7 cm
        4        +0.304 (n=3)            15.1 cm

  Monotonic on both, still rising at 4, increments shrinking (+0.15, +0.04,
  +0.09 against an sd of 0.04). One arm gives essentially nothing: with a single
  arm the positive pairs are same-arm-different-gripper, nearly identical images,
  so the loss has almost no work to do.

  A NOTE ON HOW THIS WAS READ. An intermediate version of this document said the
  arm curve saturates at 2, on the strength of the single 3-arm point sitting
  below the 2-arm one. Adding a second seed at 1 and 2 arms and pooling the three
  4-arm replicates makes the curve monotonic. The correct statement is the one
  above: arms help, and much less per unit of render cost than scenes.

0.3 WHAT THAT MEANS FOR RENDER BUDGET
  Both axes multiply render volume linearly. Over the range we can measure:
      doubling scenes    ~1.5x reduction in rotation error, repeatedly
      doubling arms      ~1.1x reduction in translation error, +0.06 pose disc
  Scenes dominate per unit of cost by a wide margin. Spend there first.

1. THE CORPUS WORTH COMMISSIONING -- "selfws_v3"
================================================================================

1.1 THE ONE CHANGE THAT MATTERS MOST: THE PROTOCOL

  selfws_v2/kitchen is TASK TRAJECTORY data. Every embodiment replays the same
  EEF path by IK, so contact happens, and each arm's contact dynamics differ ->
  the objects end up in different places. The counterfactual is exact for the
  gripper (cross-embodiment std 1.6 mm) and only approximate for the objects.
  That contamination is why the derived masks pick up the paper-towel roll, and
  why no clean in-domain object probe was possible.

  selfws_v2/no_kitchen already has the right protocol -- "move the gripper
  through the workspace", free poses, no contact -- but it is an empty room with
  a coloured wall, so it has no scene and no objects.

  selfws_v3 = the no_kitchen PROTOCOL inside the kitchen SCENES.

  Then nothing in the frame moves except the arm, the counterfactual is exact for
  everything, and the whole object-divergence caveat disappears. It is also
  cheaper to generate: no demonstrations needed, no task policy, just IK to
  sampled reachable poses. This is exactly what the research note argues for --
  embodiment-alignment data generated independently of task demonstrations -- and
  we have never actually had it with a scene in the frame.

1.2 AXES, WITH CURRENT VALUES AND TARGETS

  axis                 selfws_v2/kitchen   target        why
  -------------------  ------------------  ------------  ---------------------------
  scenes                32                 100-120       section 0.1. THE priority.
                                                         Rotation error still falling
                                                         1.5x per doubling at 24.
  arms                   6                 6-8           section 0.2. Helps, but ~1.1x
                                                         per doubling. Do not spend
                                                         render budget here; 6 already
                                                         allows a 2-arm holdout
  grippers               5                 5-7           adequate already
  cameras per scene      1                 3-5           THE known failure mode
  poses per scene      ~95 (task path)     100+ free     free poses cover the
                                                         workspace, a task path does not
  gripper open/closed   one per episode    both          currently a whole-subset flag

  ARMS: UR5e, Panda, IIWA, Jaco, Sawyer, VX300SMobile are present. Kinova3, FR3,
  xArm7, PiPER, YAM all exist in other corpora in this repo (eef_pairs has 8 arms,
  LIBERO synthetic has kinova3). Adding them costs nothing but render time and
  doubles the holdout design space.

  CAMERAS: this is the axis with direct measured evidence behind it. Feature
  displacement, normalised by a real pose change: different background 1%,
  different robot 4%, but a 40 px image shift 31% and a zoom-out 41%. The tower
  is invariant to appearance and NOT to geometry, because every corpus so far
  gave it one framing (selfws) or four tied to the background (eef_pairs). Three
  to five genuinely different camera poses per scene is the cheapest fix
  available and it targets the thing that actually breaks.

1.3 THE TWO LABELS THE SIMULATOR CAN EMIT FOR FREE AND DOESN'T

  SEGMENTATION MASKS. No dataset in the repository ships one. Experiment 1 worked
  around it by taking the per-pixel MEDIAN across the 30 embodiment renders, which
  is a robot-free image of the scene, and thresholding. That works -- it is what
  method C and D train on -- but it has two limits worth removing:
      * it captures the robot UNION the scene the robot occludes UNION objects
        that embodiment displaced differently
      * it only exists when many embodiments render the same row, so it cannot be
        computed for a single-embodiment dataset
  A real MuJoCo segmentation pass costs one extra render call. Emit per-link IDs
  if possible, not just robot/not-robot: it would let us ask whether aligning the
  GRIPPER region alone beats aligning the whole arm, which is the obvious next
  ablation and is currently unaskable.

  OBJECT POSES. selfws labels the object only through a task string that is 1:1
  with the episode, hence 1:1 with the scene. An "object identity" probe there is
  a relabelled scene probe -- it would look fine and prove nothing. Experiment 1
  had to borrow LIBERO's environment_state for metric 4, which drags a
  synthetic-to-synthetic domain gap into the number. Emitting object poses (and
  ideally object segmentation) makes the object probe in-domain and honest.

1.4 WHAT TO KEEP EXACTLY AS IT IS

  Do not change these. They are why selfws transferred to real policy frames when
  eef_pairs did not:
      * the RoboCasa renderer, camera convention and image size that the POLICY
        data uses (observation.images.<emb>.robot0_agentview_right, 180x320)
      * every embodiment of a row in the SAME parquet row, pixel-aligned
      * per-embodiment eef_state, reachable, pos_err_m, rot_err_deg. The
        reachable/pos_err pair is load-bearing: VX300SMobile reaches only 20-34%
        of poses, and treating an unreachable render as the same underlying state
        would be a labelling error, not a hard positive.

  A NOTE ON FRAMING, because it is the lesson that cost the most: eef_pairs put
  the camera so close that the arm filled the frame, the EEF sat at pixel
  (110, 33) near the top edge, and the whole workspace was a 26 x 10 x 26 cm box.
  That corpus taught appearance invariance and nothing transferable. Match the
  policy's framing, or the corpus is decorative.

2. THE OTHER GAP, WHICH IS NOT A RENDER
================================================================================
  EVALUATION FOR HELD-OUT EMBODIMENT x HELD-OUT TASK.

  visual_robust_libero/action is already the research problem in miniature:
      IIWA_Robotiq85  -> spatial suite only
      Panda_PandaGr   -> object suite only
      Panda_Rethink   -> long suite only
      UR5e_Robotiq85  -> goal suite only
  Each embodiment sees exactly one task family. The question the whole programme
  exists to answer is whether IIWA can do an `object` task using what Panda
  learned. We have the training data for that and no way to score it.

  What is missing is rollout evaluation with these embodiments on suites they
  never demonstrated. The dual-tower diffusion-policy result currently reads
  "configuration makes no difference to TRAINING loss on SEEN embodiments"
  (spread 0.0310-0.0322, while one seed moves 0.0316 -> 0.0306, so seed noise is
  60% of the between-configuration spread). That is a genuine null, but it is a
  null about fitting, not about transfer, and it will stay that way until there
  is a held-out rollout number.

  This is worth more than any new render corpus.

3. THINGS THAT SOUND USEFUL AND ARE NOT, YET
================================================================================
  MORE eef_pairs. 74 GB already, 56 embodiments, and no scene axis. Nothing to
  add until it has one, and if it had one it would be selfws_v3.

  MORE CAMERAS IN ONE KITCHEN. 56combo_8000_local_motion_27cam_kitchen_l8s0 has
  27 cameras and 1 kitchen. Cameras need to vary WITHIN scenes that also vary;
  27 views of a single kitchen cannot separate the two.

  REAL-ROBOT DATA. Premature. The synthetic-to-real question has not been reached
  -- we are still on synthetic-to-synthetic-policy-frames, and Experiment 1 just
  showed that gap is crossable when the render domain matches.

4. RECOMMENDED ORDER
================================================================================
  1. Read the scaling curves (running now, ~90 min). They set the scene and arm
     targets in 1.2 instead of guessing them.
  2. Build the held-out-embodiment rollout evaluation. Section 2. Highest value,
     no rendering.
  3. Commission selfws_v3 with the axes section 0 selected. Section 1.
  4. Re-run the dual-tower diffusion policy with the Experiment 1 D checkpoint
     frozen in, INCLUDING the branch-dropout diagnostic. The previous null used
     the eef_pairs tower, which we now know was the wrong tower; the diagnostic
     showed the policy was not reading that branch at all (dropping it 30% of the
     time changed the loss by -0.0003).
